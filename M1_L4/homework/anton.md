### One thing to think about
The real internal pull-up (~45 kΩ) is well above the ~6.6 kΩ floor; chip designers select a high resistance in the tens of kilohms range to minimize battery drain when the button is actively held down, while still providing enough voltage to reliably hold a logic HIGH against stray noise.
