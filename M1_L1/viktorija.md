## Current ideas for projects
### Inventory tracking
- Tracking physical items using RFID (optionally NFC or geo-location)
- Recording environmental conditions where items are stored
- Automatic stock-taking robot with RFID antenna that drives around warehouse and creates a map of items
### Motorcycle ride analysis
- Real-time recording of essential indicators (gear, speed, ABS, etc.) and displaying it on an app to the instructor driving behind the student
- Analysis of the ride after the lesson:
	- Mistakes made regarding physical handling of the bike
	- Traffic mistakes made (wrong speed, wrong lane, etc.)
- Real-time audio-feedback about traffic mistakes to the student via earphones during the ride
